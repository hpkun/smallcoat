C. Scalability
   Scalability is a key challenge in large-scale SAGIN
networks, where the number of UAVs and other nodes may
increase significantly. To address this, many studies have
turned to Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) methods for
their ability to handle large, dynamic environments.
   Single-agent DRL has been successfully applied to address
the scalability challenge in SAGIN. In [22], the authors first
established a SAGIN architecture based on Software-Defined
Networking (SDN) and Network Function Virtualization
(NFV) and designed a resource scheduling and computation
offloading algorithm using Deep Q-Network (DQN). The
Asynchronous Advantage Actor-Critic (A3C) algorithm was
employed in [23] to reduce the action space and accelerate
convergence in SAGIN-assisted vehicular networks, improving
rewards and system efficiency.
